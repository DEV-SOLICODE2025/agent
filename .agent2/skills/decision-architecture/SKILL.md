# Skill: Architectural Decision Record (ADR)

## Objective
Track and document architectural decisions.

## Actions

### Action 1: Identify Decision
- What is being decided?
- Why is it necessary?

### Action 2: Consider Alternatives
- List at least 2 alternatives.
- Explain trade-offs.

### Action 3: Final Decision
- Justify chosen approach.

### Action 4: Impact Analysis
- Performance impact
- Scalability impact
- Security impact

## Output

Create file:

docs/decisions/ADR-[number].md

Format:

- Context
- Decision
- Alternatives
- Consequences

Trace required:
Action executed: Architectural Decision Record
